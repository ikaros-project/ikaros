## Development Notes

The week is centered on making `ConvolutionalVariationalAutoEncoder` experiments easier to measure rather than widening the module surface in unrelated directions. After a brief devlog maintenance commit, the repository gained hierarchy plotting support, a held-out moving-blob evaluation path, and a broader family of moving-blob trajectory tests. The generated artifacts were then moved under `UserData`, which tightened the project’s own convention that experiment outputs belong outside the repository root.

Most of the remaining work deepened the quality of CVAE diagnostics and training control. Reconstruction reporting became more explicit through per-channel loss values, live top-down usefulness metrics, and absolute reconstruction error metrics. The module then gained an optional latent decorrelation penalty and Bernoulli reconstruction loss support, with the documentation updated alongside those changes. Taken together, those commits made the module easier to inspect as a probabilistic model instead of only as a black-box reconstruction pipeline.

The evaluation surface broadened again over the weekend through MNIST-focused tooling. New scripts prepared MNIST data, measured nearest-neighbour behavior in top-code space, added a linear classifier probe, and extended those probes down to level-one latent representations. That gave the week a second evaluation track: moving-blob hierarchy behavior on one side and latent-structure probing on the other.

The week closes with two smaller but concrete maintenance results. `ERRORS.md` captured the optional session logging warning and other CVAE-related surprises observed during local runs, and the kernel gained periodic tick printing together with tests for valid and invalid interval handling. By the end of the week, Ikaros had a better instrumented CVAE workflow, cleaner experiment-output placement, and a slightly clearer batch-execution surface.
