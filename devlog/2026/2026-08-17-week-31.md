## Development Notes

The week opened with maintenance rather than a new subsystem. A devlog update landed first, followed by a refresh of the `PlanarArm_example.ikg` layout and a dedicated interface document for `PlanarArm`. That work gave the recently added arm module a clearer presentation surface and made its inputs, outputs, and parameters easier to read directly from the repository.

Most of the week then turned to `Nucleus`. Several commits tightened the module's numerical and behavioral contract: the threshold activation path moved to a burst-envelope state machine, parameter scaling became explicit with respect to tick duration, shunting inhibition and leak strength were constrained to remain non-negative, and non-finite updates were contained instead of propagating unchecked. Documentation moved with those runtime changes through clearer descriptions of alpha, delta, and fan-in normalization, and the module gained a comparative dynamics demo together with a homeostatic adaptation mechanism. By the end of that series, `Nucleus` had become both more explicit in its equations and more controlled in its runtime behavior.

The closing push shifted to `RingWorld`, where the work broadened from documentation into protocol design. The manual was expanded, a PDF version was added and updated, and the protocol format itself gained version declarations, experiment metadata, trial templates, controlled ordering, weighted choice, generalized random values, labeled phases and trials, explanatory notes, and measurement units. Those protocol features then turned into execution support through bounded criterion training, a scheduler, response analysis, complete experiment recording, and a protocol-aware dashboard. The week ends with `RingWorld` looking less like an isolated simulation and more like a structured experiment environment with its own authoring and review tools.
