## Development Notes

Work recorded under `ikaros-project` extended the existing latent-code studies with spatially organized, unsupervised prototype maps. The runner imported three trained direct dense VAE checkpoints, checked their codes and reconstruction against saved Ikaros output, and standardized the active latent dimensions using training data. Labels entered only after training to assign digit readouts to map cells. The comparison therefore kept unsupervised representation learning separate from its supervised classification readout.

The study used 100- and 256-cell maps. At 256 cells, the frozen SOM reached 90.61 +/- 0.25% test accuracy, compared with 90.81 +/- 0.42% for joint SOM training. At 100 cells, their mean accuracies were identical. Joint SOM training increased continuous reconstruction error by 16% at 256 cells. Its neighbourhood objective improved topographic ordering relative to joint training without topology, but neither joint condition matched the frozen map's lower topographic error. Together, these measurements showed why a falling commitment loss alone was insufficient evidence of a better representation.

The committed report recorded five passing numerical and protocol tests, a small-data smoke run, and full-data import checks. It also identified the experiment as a warm-started adaptation with fixed settings and a previously inspected MNIST test set, rather than an untouched final benchmark. The results supported retaining frozen VAE plus SOM as the baseline without incorporating joint SOM fine-tuning into the production module. The week's other commits added the preceding weekly devlog and merged the repository branch; that earlier week's development is covered by its existing entry.
