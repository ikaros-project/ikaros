## Development Notes

Work recorded under `ikaros-project` expanded the CVAE training surface beyond reconstruction and latent decorrelation. Paired augmented views could now encourage consistent latent means, while soft prototypes and VQ-style updates offered alternative ways to organize the representation. A state-loading fix ensured that restored learned weights were handled correctly, an important practical detail for experiments that train a model and then extract frozen codes in a separate run.

The week also made comparison more systematic. Seeded MNIST sweeps and deterministic refinements documented selected settings, and a fully downsampled five-level hierarchy tested progressive spatial reduction. A direct dense VAE mode then provided a smaller architecture without convolutional feature stages or hidden layers. Its mechanism and latent-size studies separated the effects of the reconstruction objective, KL weight, consistency, decorrelation, and prototype terms. Optional hard-concrete gates and their schedules added explicit control over latent feature use, with evaluation scripts and results accompanying the implementation.

The evaluation work distinguished unsupervised representation learning from downstream classification. Full centered-MNIST runs used frozen VAE codes for nearest-neighbour, fixed-prototype, and trainable prototype-mixture comparisons. The recorded 200-center trainable mixture matched single-neighbour accuracy at about 96.8% while storing 300 times fewer reference vectors; larger mixtures extended the capacity comparison. These results describe the documented centered-MNIST protocol, and labels were used for the downstream classifier rather than VAE training. The smaller parameter screens retained their separate limitations, including repeated selection on a small validation subset.

Several runtime changes addressed the mechanics of running these experiments. Relative state paths now resolve from the invocation directory, CLI overrides respect the model's exposed parameter boundary, and offline logging warnings explain that local execution continues. The week opened with a `-P` shorthand and a regression model for tick-interval printing. Native PGM support broadened image input and output, including the grayscale data used by the MNIST workflows.

Issue reporting moved from the local `ERRORS.md` file to documented bot-based GitHub procedures, with topic labels and a complete chat suggestion as the fallback when the bot is unavailable. The final commit corrected statistics views: bar graphs, box plots, and histograms convert boolean options explicitly, and graph range selection gives computed ranges precedence over configured fallback bounds.
